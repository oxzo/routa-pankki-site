Insurance

The idea of insurance is simple: you pay a little every month so that one big loss doesn’t capsize your finances. Prices are illustrative – the final insurance premium depends on what you insure.

Routa Kotivakuutus home insurance

Home insurance for damage to your home and belongings. Monthly premium €9–24/month by home and cover chosen.
