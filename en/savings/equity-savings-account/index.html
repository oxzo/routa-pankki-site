Routa Osakesäästötili equity savings account

Tax-advantaged account for shares and ETFs. Monthly fee €0, custody €0. Use it for monthly saving or lump-sum investments.

  • Monthly fee €0
  • Custody €0
  • Trading per price list
  • Finnish and European equity markets

The Routa Osakesäästötili is a state-supported savings vehicle for shares and ETFs. You can invest up to €50,000. Tax on returns is deferred until withdrawal.

Investing from the account

You can trade Finnish and European shares and ETFs. A monthly savings plan suits beginners. Trading costs follow the price list.

Taxation

Returns and capital gains from the account are taxed only when you withdraw. This can be advantageous over a long investment horizon. We do not provide tax advice; consult a tax adviser.
